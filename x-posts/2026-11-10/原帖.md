## 5. Alex Albert（@alexalbert__）· 2026-11-10 03:08 CST · 相关

> I really love using Fable 5.2. On big, open-ended work it feels like a major step above Opus 5.5. 
>
> A few tips for getting the most out of it:
> - Ask what’s wrong with your idea before it builds anything. It’s great at brainstorming and will push back
> - Start on med/high effort, and save xhigh for hardest problems
> - Tell it up front what needs your approval (e.g. deleting things) and that the rest is its call
> - Give it a whole project instead of a next step and let it run for hours. This is where it shines
